Why Tacos Los Poblanos

Tijuana-style tacos, made for the street

Tacos Los Poblanos has served Tijuana-style tacos in Los Angeles since 2013 — meats and chorizo grilled over charcoal, served on handmade tortillas.

The menu stays short and focused on four favourites: al pastor tacos, cabeza tacos, mulitas and quesadillas. Charcoal-grilled and made for eating on the go.
