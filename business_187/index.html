Why Big Easy Soul Kitchen

Soul food, Cajun and Creole, made for the street

Soul food is made to be eaten hot and shared — gumbo over rice, fried catfish smothered in étouffée, wings and red beans.

The menu stays short and focused on four favourites: the catfish and étouffée combo, meat and seafood gumbo, Cajun fried wings and Creole red beans and white rice.
