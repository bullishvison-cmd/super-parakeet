QUALIFICATION RULES APPLIED
- Genuine active small US food business with a Google Maps / Business Profile listing.
- NO official website: Maps website field, exact name + city + address search, and
  social profiles checked. Facebook/Instagram alone is NOT a website.
  A missing Maps website field alone was never treated as proof.
- A publicly listed, verified business email with an exact source URL.
  No invented, guessed, or pattern-generated addresses. Personal-format addresses
  were used ONLY where published as the business contact on ThreeBestRated
  (Las Gorditas LLC: mrpjmc857@hotmail.com).
- No invented menu items, prices, hours, reviews, ratings, or awards.
  Menu sections show only visitor-reported items or described specialties,
  labelled as such, with no prices. Conflicting hours are flagged in the demo,
  never silently picked.

PHOTOS
